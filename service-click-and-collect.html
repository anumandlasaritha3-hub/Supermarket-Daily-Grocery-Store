Overview

About Click & Collect

Click & Collect lets you shop online and skip the queues. Build your basket at home, pick a time and we will have your bag packed and waiting.

There is no delivery fee, and your order is kept fresh at our pickup counter until you arrive.

What You Get

Everything included

  • No fees

    Collecting is completely free.

  • Ready in 2 hours

    Same-day pickup on most orders.

  • Pickup counter

    A quick, queue-free handover.

  • Fresh storage

    Chilled items are kept cool.

  • Held for 4 hours

    Plenty of time to arrive.

  • Easy substitutions

    We contact you if something is out.
