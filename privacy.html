1Overview

DailyKart respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it and the choices you have when you shop with us.

2Information We Collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number and password.
  • Order details: delivery address, items ordered, delivery notes and shopping lists.
  • Messages: anything you send through our contact forms or support channels.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and browser type, pages visited and approximate location based on your IP address.
  • Preferences such as theme (light or dark) and layout direction (LTR or RTL), stored in your browser.

3How We Use Your Information

  • To process, pack and deliver your orders, and to send order updates.
  • To manage your account, shopping lists and order history.
  • To answer your questions and provide customer support.
  • To send offers and news, only where you have agreed to receive them.
  • To keep our website secure, prevent fraud and improve our service.

4Who We Share It With

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only when needed to run our service:

  • Delivery partners who need your name, address and phone number to reach you.
  • Payment providers who process your payments securely.
  • Service providers for hosting, analytics and communication, under strict confidentiality.
  • Authorities when required by law.

5Cookies & Local Storage

We use small files and browser storage to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and understand how the site is used.

TypePurpose
EssentialSign-in, cart and security features that the site needs to work.
PreferencesRemembers your theme, direction and saved email if you ask us to.
AnalyticsHelps us see which pages are useful so we can improve them.

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings, though some features may stop working.

6Data Security & Retention

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your information. No online service is completely risk-free, so please use a strong, unique password.

We keep your information only as long as needed to provide our services, meet legal and accounting requirements, and resolve disputes. After that, it is deleted or anonymised.

7Your Rights & Choices

You can ask us to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct details that are inaccurate or out of date.
  • Delete your account and personal information, where the law allows.
  • Stop sending you marketing messages, using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

You may also have rights under applicable data protection laws in India, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

8Children's Privacy

DailyKart is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us data, please contact us and we will remove it.

9Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as our services change. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for important changes, let you know by email or on the website.
