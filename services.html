FAQ

Questions, answered

Which areas do you deliver to?

We deliver across our service areas. Enter your pincode at checkout to confirm availability.

Can I combine services?

Yes. You can keep a Weekly Fresh Box and still place Express orders whenever you need.

How do I pay?

We accept UPI, cards, net banking and cash on delivery.

What if something is not fresh?

Tell us within 24 hours and we will replace it or refund you.

Is there a minimum order?

There is no minimum, but delivery is free on orders above ₹499.
