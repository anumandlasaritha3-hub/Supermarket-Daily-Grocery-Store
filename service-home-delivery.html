Overview

About Home Delivery

Home Delivery brings the whole DailyKart range to your doorstep. Fill your basket with fruits, vegetables, dairy, pantry staples and household items, then choose a delivery slot that suits your day.

Every order is packed by our team, with fresh and chilled items kept cool until they reach you. It is the easiest way to handle your weekly shop without carrying a single bag.
